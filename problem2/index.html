Problem 2 — Coupled Pendulums

Horizontal spring attached at 3/4 of each massless rod.

Linearized:
θ̈₁+(g/l)θ₁+(9k/16m)(θ₁−θ₂)=0
θ̈₂+(g/l)θ₂+(9k/16m)(θ₂−θ₁)=0

Nonlinear:
θ̈₁+(g/l)sinθ₁+(9k/16m)cosθ₁(sinθ₁−sinθ₂)=0
θ̈₂+(g/l)sinθ₂+(9k/16m)cosθ₂(sinθ₂−sinθ₁)=0

Compare the normal modes: θ₁=θ₂ means almost no spring deformation; θ₁=−θ₂ stretches the spring strongly and oscillates faster.
